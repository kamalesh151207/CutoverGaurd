DECISION THEORY

The Latest Responsible Moment (LRM)

The Latest Responsible Moment is the latest transition date that can be considered operationally feasible before material holding costs, inventory obsolescence, or plant line locks create unacceptable risk.

Why the LRM is not the recommended date

A common planning error is waiting until the last second to exhaust every last unit of old inventory. While this eliminates scrap, it destroys supply-chain resilience: any supplier delay immediately halts production.

CutoverGuard separates the two: The Recommended Window (12–16 Dec) preserves a safety buffer for lead-time jitter, while the Latest Responsible Moment (19 Dec) marks the strict boundary after which holding costs and ECN implementation deadlines begin causing severe cost escalation.

Recommended Date: Mathematical minimum of total transition cost.
LRM: The drop-dead date before holding excess inventory becomes riskier than minor scrap.
