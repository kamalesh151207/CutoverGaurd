The Latest Responsible Moment (LRM)

The Latest Responsible Moment is the latest transition date that can be considered operationally feasible before material holding costs, inventory obsolescence, or plant line locks create unacceptable risk.

Why the LRM is not the recommended date

A common planning error is waiting until the last second to exhaust every last unit of old inventory. While this eliminates scrap, it destroys supply-chain resilience: any supplier delay immediately halts production.

CutoverGuard separates the two: The Recommended Window (12–16 Dec) preserves a safety buffer for lead-time jitter, while the Latest Responsible Moment (19 Dec) marks the strict boundary after which holding costs and ECN implementation deadlines begin causing severe cost escalation.

JURY SUMMARY (Under 10 Seconds)

Earliest Feasible: New parts are inspected and ready.
Recommended Date: Mathematical minimum of total transition cost.
LRM: The drop-dead date before holding excess inventory becomes riskier than minor scrap.

INTEGRITY ARCHITECTURE

Trust is engineered into the decision.

Industrial operations cannot risk trusting opaque black-box outputs. CutoverGuard enforces a three-tier integrity architecture ensuring deterministic mathematics, reproducibility, and tamper-evident audit history.

LAYER 01

Numerical Verification

All core quantities, dates, shortage units, and costs originate in deterministic formulas. AI is strictly prohibited from inventing or hallucinating numbers.

LAYER 02

Versioned Decision Engine

Every cutover calculation locks the exact rule kernel version, parameter weights, constraint snapshot, and random seed to ensure bit-level reproducibility.

LAYER 03

Hash-Chained Audit Trail

Every calculation state and human approval is cryptographically linked to the previous block's SHA-256 hash. Any retroactive alteration breaks the verification chain.

HUMAN GOVERNANCE

The system never auto-commits production cutovers. Recommendations require explicit verification and sign-off by an authorized manufacturing planner.
